# Multi-Platform Distribution Strategy

## Platform Sequencing (Recommended Order)
1. **Reddit** — post first, highest organic reach ceiling, sets the "proof" narrative
2. **Twitter/X** — thread summarizing the Reddit post + link (drives Reddit upvotes from followers)
3. **Discord** — share in relevant servers or own community channels (warm audience)
4. **Telegram** — broadcast to channel subscribers (highest open rate ~70-90%)

---

## Twitter/X Strategy

### Thread Format for Distribution
- Tweet 1: Hook — restate the Reddit title as a punchy statement or question
- Tweet 2-5: Expand the story with numbered insights ("3 things I learned...")
- Tweet 6: The result/outcome with specific numbers
- Tweet 7: CTA + Reddit link ("Full breakdown in comments on Reddit:")
- End with a poll or open question to boost engagement signals

### Optimal Posting Times (EST)
- Weekdays: 8-9am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm
- Avoid weekends for B2B content; fine for consumer/indie products

### Hashtag Strategy
- Max 2-3 hashtags per tweet
- Use: #buildinpublic #indiehacker #saas #solofounder
- Never in the middle of text — end of tweet only

## Discord Distribution

### Own Server / Workspace Channels
- Post to grok channel as the primary distribution log
- Cross-reference discord-agent for posting to external servers

### External Server Targeting
- Find #self-promo or #show-and-tell channels in niche Discord servers
- Always read channel rules before posting
- Lead with value: share insight first, mention product second
- Engage in the server for 24h before posting if possible

---

## Telegram Broadcast

### Channel Posts
- Telegram-agent handles workspace Telegram channel
- Format: Short punchy message (3-5 lines) + link
- Telegram supports bold/italic formatting — use sparingly for emphasis
- Best time: 9-11am and 7-9pm local time for your audience

### Growth Hacks
- Pin milestone posts to channel
- Run "comment with your biggest challenge" CTAs to drive Telegram group discussions

## Campaign Templates

### Product Launch Campaign
- Day 0: Reddit r/SideProject launch post
- Day 0 +2h: Twitter thread with Reddit link
- Day 1: Discord #self-promo drop
- Day 1 EOD: Telegram broadcast
- Day 3: Reddit r/Entrepreneur "lessons from launch week" post
- Day 7: Twitter/X recap thread with numbers

### Content Marketing Campaign (no product launch)
- Monday: Reddit value post (how-to or story)
- Tuesday: Twitter thread version
- Wednesday: Discord engagement (ask a question, share insight)
- Thursday: Telegram broadcast of best-performing content
- Friday: Reddit follow-up or AMA-style comment thread
