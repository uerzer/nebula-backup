# Growth Copywriting Frameworks

## The 5 Hook Archetypes

### 1. The Contrarian
"Everyone says [common belief]. They're wrong. Here's what actually works."
- Works best: Twitter, Reddit r/Entrepreneur
- Risk: Medium (can alienate if not backed by substance)

### 2. The Number Promise
"[Specific number] [outcome] in [timeframe] — here's exactly how."
- Examples: "14 paying customers in 8 days", "From 0 to $2K MRR in 6 weeks"
- Works best: All platforms — universally high CTR
- Rule: Numbers MUST be real. Never inflate.

### 3. The Vulnerable Admission
"I almost quit [thing] last month. What happened next changed how I work."
- Works best: Reddit, Discord, LinkedIn
- Triggers: Empathy, curiosity, relatability

### 4. The Question Hook
"Why do [successful people/companies] do [unexpected thing]?"
- Works best: Twitter, Reddit
- Drives: Comments and debate (algorithmic gold)

### 5. The Specific Lesson
"The one thing I wish someone told me before [common experience]."
- Works best: Reddit, Telegram
- Drives: Saves, bookmarks, shares

## Body Copy Rules

### Sentence Structure
- Vary sentence length deliberately: short punches after long buildup.
- Write at 7th grade reading level (use Hemingway App standard).
- One idea per paragraph. Two sentences max before a line break.

### Credibility Anchors (insert naturally)
- Specific dates: "On March 4th, I tried..."
- Specific numbers: "We had 847 signups but only 12 converted"
- Named tools/platforms: "Using Supabase + Vercel, we shipped in..."
- Failure details: "This approach cost us 3 weeks and $400"

### Emotional Arc Structure
1. Pain (relatable problem)
2. Attempt (what you tried that failed)
3. Discovery (the insight or pivot)
4. Result (specific, measurable)
5. Offer (here's how you can do this too)

## CTA Copy Bank

### Soft CTAs (Reddit-safe)
- "Happy to go deeper on any of this in the comments."
- "Drop your biggest [problem] below — let's troubleshoot."
- "If this was helpful, I've written more about it [here/in my profile]."
- "Built a tool that handles this automatically — link in comments if anyone wants it."

### Direct CTAs (Twitter/Discord/Telegram)
- "Try it free: [link]"
- "DM me 'GROWTH' and I'll send the full template."
- "Grab the checklist: [link] — took me 6 months to build this."
- "First 10 people to reply get a free audit."

## Engagement Optimization

### The Comment Magnet Technique
End posts with an open question directly tied to the reader's situation:
- "What's your current biggest bottleneck with [topic]?"
- "Has anyone else tried [approach]? What happened?"
- "Would you do this differently?"

### The Edit Bump
- After 30-60 min on Reddit, add "EDIT: Wow, didn't expect this response. Happy to answer questions — ask below."
- This bumps the post in "new" and signals activity to the algorithm.

### Timing Windows for Maximum Visibility
- Reddit: Post 30 min before peak hour so you're climbing during peak
- Twitter: Post ON the hour for scheduled discovery
- Telegram: Post when open rates are highest (morning commute + evening)
